## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, tactile, and culturally literate digital salon. Designed for spontaneous connection, collective timing, and shared cultural rituals, it rejects sterile modern corporate SaaS conventions in favor of a warm, human, and confident atmosphere—the feel of a sunlit living room with deep conversation and vinyl playing.

### Brand Archetype & Emotion
- **The Warm Host:** Grounded, gracious, welcoming, rejecting cold high-tech aesthetics.
- **The Contemporary Curator:** Confident typographic scale, asymmetric editorial weight, and uncompromising composition.
- **The Synchronous Pulse:** Energetic and immediate, punctuated by moments of electric intensity without resort to artificial glows or gradient gimmicks.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Editorial Post-Modernism:** Organic warmth paired with sharp, intentional structure. Clean flat planes of tinted cream, generous negative space, strong ink-dense typography, and punchy, unblended color hits.
- **Anti-AI Philosophy:** Zero ethereal multi-stop purple gradients, no floating iridescent glass spheres, and no automated sparkle icons. Depth is created through confident spacing, paper-like tonal layering, and direct contrast.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in tactile warmth, anchored by deep inky indigo on warm cream, and energized with vivid electric strikes.

### Palette Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#F8F6F1`):** Warm cream. The foundational paper layer across the entire interface. It eliminates eye fatigue and sets a domestic, tactile baseline.
- **Ink / Main Text (`#17172B`):** Deep almost-black indigo. Rich, dense, and sharper than charcoal, delivering punchy editorial contrast.
- **Primary / Vivid Electric Indigo (`#5146E5`):** A decisive, high-frequency purple-blue used for core actions, active statuses, and selected states. Used strictly as a solid color—never blended into gradients.
- **Secondary / Warm Coral (`#FF796D`):** An energetic, social accent representing spontaneous moments, live notifications, real-time presence, and primary highlights.
- **Supporting Lavender (`#E0DEFA`):** Soft, structural surface tint used for inner containers, tags, and subtle segment boundaries.
- **Supporting Muted Sage (`#A3B899`):** A grounding, calm secondary accent indicating settled states, harmony, confirmations, and ambient metadata.
- **Card / Surface Container (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp, flat white paper layer for foreground cards and focus surfaces resting atop the warm cream canvas.

### Color Rules
- Surfaces must remain solid or tinted. No transparent frosted blurs or multi-color gradients.
- High color contrast is mandatory: deep indigo (`#17172B`) handles all semantic text hierarchies down to secondary subheads; muted states use a 65% opacity tint of indigo rather than generic gray.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, relying on drastic scale contrasts, ultra-heavy weights, and tight tracking to produce an editorial identity.

### Editorial Hierarchy & Rhythm
- **Oversized Numerals & Time Markers (`display-numeral`):** Massive numeric data, live countdowns, timestamps, and order indices are set extra bold to anchor user attention like editorial magazine callouts.
- **Tight Headings:** High tracking compression (`-0.03em` to `-0.05em`) binds words together into architectural visual blocks.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Small uppercase labels (`label-caps`) provide sharp counter-tension to wide-open body layouts and oversized display elements.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize intentional asymmetry, expansive whitespace, and distinct rhythm over dense tabular symmetry.

### Grid & Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column asymmetric grid. Columns are paired unevenly (e.g., 5-column editorial manifesto block against a 7-column staggered interaction feed). Minimum desktop margins are `space-4xl` (64px) to preserve breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `space-2xl` (48px) gutters. Cards reflow to stacked dynamic widths.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px outside margins. Sections avoid symmetric card stacks by using off-canvas peek previews and varied vertical heights.

### High-Whitespace Cadence
- Content sections are spaced aggressively using `space-3xl` and `space-4xl` intervals. Negative space functions as a structural frame for oversized display typography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through paper-like surface layering, sharp tonal shifts, and low-offset ink shadows. Glassmorphism and ambient blur halos are prohibited.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Base Tier (Ground):** The raw canvas in `#F8F6F1` (Warm Cream).
2. **Layer 1 (Card & Paper Surfaces):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces sitting directly on cream, framed with an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.08)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Lifted / Interactive Modules):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp, low-diffusion ink drop: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.04)`.
4. **Layer 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Pure `#FFFFFF` paired with an ink-tinted backdrop scrim: `rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.45)`. Elevated with `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, geometric curvature (`roundedness: 2`). Radii soften interactions without becoming toy-like or pill-diluted.

### Shape Scales
- **Inputs, Buttons, & Small Chips:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Crisp and stable.
- **Content Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) radius. Creates a natural organic boundary that sets items apart from the canvas.
- **Modals, Sheets, & Hero Frames (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) radius. Expressive framing for prominent social moments.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved solely for live indicator pips, user avatars, and status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Electric Indigo (`#5146E5`) fill, pure white text, 8px border radius, bold typography (`label-md`). Hover state: `#4338CA`. Active state: subtle scale down (`0.98`).
- **Moment / Accent:** Warm Coral (`#FF796D`) fill, deep indigo text (`#17172B`). Reserved for real-time joins, countdown engagements, and critical primary calls.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.15)`, text in `#17172B`. Hover state brings a flat fill of `#E0DEFA` at 40% opacity.

### Cards & Modules (Anti-Generic AI Patterns)
- **Structure:** Crisp white background with an asymmetrical padding balance (e.g., larger bottom and side margins, tight top margin).
- **Accents:** Cards use solid vertical or top accent stripes in Muted Sage (`#A3B899`) or Lavender (`#E0DEFA`) instead of perimeter gradients.
- **Numerals:** Time indices, user rankings, and counters sit oversized in `display-numeral` on the upper perimeter of the card.

### Chips & Tags
- **Presence Chips:** Flat Soft Lavender (`#E0DEFA`) background, deep indigo text (`#17172B`), `label-caps` typography, 6px radius.
- **Active / Live Indicator:** Small 8px circular dot in Warm Coral (`#FF796D`) accompanied by tracking label in `#17172B`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Cream-tinted white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5px` stroke in `rgba(23, 23, 43, 0.14)`, `0.5rem` radius, 16px horizontal padding.
- **Focus State:** Stroke sharpens directly to Electric Indigo (`#5146E5`) with zero diffused outer glow.

### Real-Time Synchronous Components
- **The 'Moment' Bar:** Persistent horizontal status rail displaying who is currently present. Warm cream backdrop with avatars framed in crisp white rings, terminated by a Coral action button.
- **Typographic Live Ticker:** Ultra-bold editorial text strings cycling on an unbordered, flat Lavender strip to declare communal occurrences.
